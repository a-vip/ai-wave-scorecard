## 2. Week-by-Week Run of Show & Worksheets

### Week 1: First Principles & The 5-Question Shelf-Life Filter
- **Core Objective:** Eliminate AI overwhelm and prune tool sprawl down to a **Core-3 Stack**.
- **Part 1 (30 mins) — How Frontier Models Actually Work:**
  - Token prediction vs. reasoning models (`o3` / `Claude 3.7+` / `Gemini 2.5 Pro`), context windows, temperature, and why models hallucinate when asked to retrieve and synthesize simultaneously.
- **Part 2 (30 mins) — The BCG Tool-Sprawl Audit:**
  - Live exercise: Every participant lists every AI subscription and browser tab they use.
  - Apply the **5-Question Shelf-Life Filter**:
    1. Does this solve a weekly bottleneck costing ≥2 hours?
    2. Is this a thin wrapper that frontier models will absorb in 90 days?
    3. Can I export my prompts, context, and data in plain Markdown/JSON?
    4. Does it respect client confidentiality and zero-training privacy settings?
    5. Does it belong in my **Core 3** (1 Frontier Reasoning Model + 1 Grounded Knowledge Workspace + 1 Automation Trigger)?
- **Part 3 (30 mins) — Hot-Seat Diagnostic Teardowns:**
  - Review each participant's AI Wave Scorecard Zone and lock in their **#1 90-Day Workflow Target**.
- **Week 1 Deliverable:** Audited Core-3 AI Stack + 90-Day Bottleneck Map.

---

### Week 2: Context Architecture & Grounded Knowledge Systems
- **Core Objective:** Replace one-off "chatting" with reusable **4-Layer Context Scaffolds** and grounded RAG workspaces.
- **Part 1 (30 mins) — The 4-Layer Context Architecture:**
  - **Layer 1 (Mandate & Role Calibration):** Setting domain expertise, objective, and anti-cliché constraints.
  - **Layer 2 (Grounded Context Block):** Feeding source documents, tone rules, and structural constraints.
  - **Layer 3 (Execution Protocol):** Step-by-step chain-of-thought instructions (Quote extraction first → synthesis second).
  - **Layer 4 (Verification Rubric):** Forcing the model to flag unverified claims and output a human verification checklist.
- **Part 2 (40 mins) — Building Your Personal Context Library:**
  - Participants use the live **Context Architect** (`https://aviperera.com/ai-academy/#interactive-lab`) to build 3 master templates for their weekly work (e.g., Executive Synthesis, Strategy Red-Teaming, Client Deliverable Drafting).
- **Part 3 (20 mins) — Grounded Workspaces (NotebookLM / Claude Projects / Custom GPTs):**
  - How to organize reference PDFs, transcripts, and style guides so AI answers strictly from your trusted corpus.
- **Week 2 Deliverable:** 3 Production-Ready Context Scaffolds + 1 Grounded Knowledge Project.

---

### Week 3: Verification, Privacy & Meaningful Human Control (MHC)
- **Core Objective:** Install professional-grade accuracy, legal/policy compliance, and data safety guardrails.
- **Part 1 (30 mins) — The 3-Gate Verification Protocol:**
  - **Gate 1 (Input Hygiene):** Redacting PII/confidential identifiers using the Red/Amber/Green data rubric.
  - **Gate 2 (Adversarial Cross-Examination):** Using a second model or Socratic prompt to attack assumptions and verify citations in the first model's output.
  - **Gate 3 (Meaningful Human Control Sign-Off):** The 60-second human audit checklist before any work goes to a client, board, or regulator.
- **Part 2 (30 mins) — Regulatory & Institutional Literacy (Avi’s Signature Edge):**
  - Plain-English translation of EU AI Act risk tiers, UNESCO principles, and enterprise shadow-AI policies.
- **Part 3 (30 mins) — Live Red-Team Lab:**
  - Participants run a high-stakes document through the 3-Gate Verification Protocol live.
- **Week 3 Deliverable:** Personal or Team AI Verification & Data Governance SOP.

---

### Week 4: Multi-Step Workflows, Agent Pipelines & Demo Day
- **Core Objective:** Connect your Context Scaffolds into an automated or semi-automated multi-step pipeline and capture reviews.
- **Part 1 (30 mins) — Chained Workflows vs. Autonomous Agents:**
  - The architectural rule: Never automate a messy process until the manual 4-Layer Context Scaffold works 9/10 times.
  - Building a 3-step pipeline: *Ingest/Monitor → Extract & Verify → Draft Executive Output*.
- **Part 2 (45 mins) — Participant Demo Day (5 mins per person):**
  - Each participant shares screen for 3–5 minutes to show their before/after workflow and hours saved per week.
- **Part 3 (15 mins) — Graduation, 90-Day Roadmap & Live Review Capture:**
  - Direct every participant to `https://aviperera.com/ai-academy/#authority-reviews` during the final 5 minutes of the call to submit their verified review while their win is fresh!
